Why BMI Matters

Body Mass Index helps estimate whether your weight is within a healthy range for your height. While BMI is not perfect, it is still one of the most commonly used health indicators worldwide.

Many people ignore small lifestyle changes until problems start appearing slowly — low energy, poor sleep, stress eating, or lack of physical activity. Tracking BMI regularly can help build awareness before health issues become serious.

Healthy Daily Habits

  • Drink enough water throughout the day
  • Try walking at least 20–30 minutes daily
  • Avoid heavy late-night meals regularly
  • Sleep consistently instead of randomly
  • Reduce excessive sugary drinks and snacks
